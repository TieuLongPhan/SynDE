Chemistry Support Boundary & Standard Warnings

Initial Support Boundaries (v2.0)

  • Supported Elements: Closed-shell organic graphs containing H, B, C, N, O, F, Si, P, S, Cl, Br, and I.
  • Formal Charges: Neutral and common ±1 formal-charge states.
  • π-Electron Environment: Concentrated C/N/O/S/P/B systems in common organic molecules.
  • Out of Scope: Metals, organometallics, radicals, excited states, and intermolecular interaction-energy claims without pose generation (these will produce structured warnings/unsupported statuses).

Standardized warning codes

UNSUPPORTED_ELEMENT UNSUPPORTED_VALENCE OPEN_SHELL_NOT_SUPPORTED PI_ELECTRON_ASSIGNMENT_AMBIGUOUS FRONTIER_GAP_TOO_SMALL NONFINITE_PARTIAL_CHARGE REACTION_MAPPING_MISSING REACTION_NOT_BALANCED GEOMETRY_REQUIRED OUTSIDE_CALIBRATION_DOMAIN
* Models running outside their calibration bounds retain raw descriptor exports but will not issue uncalibrated physical energy values.
